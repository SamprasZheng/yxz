## Where this sits in the 2026 Taiwan AESA lineage (verifiable context)

The XT-144 lab-era X-band AESA work is one node in Taiwan's academic→commercial phased-array lineage. Its commercial state-of-the-art descendant class in 2026 is the fielded **[[entities/tron-future-tech|Tron Future]] T.SpaceRouter** Ka-band AESA satcom-on-the-move terminal (1024-element TX + 1024-element RX, DL 17.8–20.2 / UL 27.5–30 GHz, EIRP >36 dBW, <100 W; Foxconn-EV connected-vehicle trials) — i.e. the same "strong-upstream-foundry, emerging-midstream-integrator" arc captured in [[concepts/aesa]] and [[synthesis/leo-taiwan-odc-gap]]. This lab node predates and thematically anchors that integrator arc; it is context, not an identity claim about any specific product ([Tron Future — Space Tech](https://www.tronfuture.com/solutions/space-tech/), accessed 2026-07-24).
