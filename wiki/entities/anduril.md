# Anduril Industries

US defense-technology company (founded **2017**, HQ Costa Mesa, California) building AI-driven autonomous hardware — drones, counter-drone systems, cruise missiles, underwater vehicles, surveillance towers — unified by a single software layer. Co-founded by **Palmer Luckey** (Oculus founder, ousted from Facebook 2017) with Trae Stephens, Matt Grimm, Brian Schimpf (CEO), and Joe Chen. In the six-region defense-tech map ([[synthesis/techno-industrial-state-defense-tech-six-region]]) Anduril is the **second US archetype** alongside [[entities/palantir]]: where Palantir is the *profitable, software-margin* prime, Anduril is the *loss-making, hardware-scaling* prime — the two faces of the same 2025–26 [[concepts/technological-republic|re-coupling]] of frontier engineering to state purpose.

## The software layer — the Palantir parallel

- **Lattice OS** — an AI command-and-control / sensor-fusion mesh that ingests any sensor and autonomously tasks any effector; the software-defined heart of every Anduril product and the direct analogue to Palantir's Gotham/AIP, but built to *fly and shoot* rather than to analyse. Lattice is what lets Anduril sell "the operating system for defense," not a catalogue of point products — the same knowledge-first / integration-first logic as [[concepts/domain-specific-llm-agents]] applied to kill-chains.

## Financial scale + the profitability contrast (fact-checked 2026-09-18)

- **Revenue:** ~$1.5B (2024) → **≈$2.2B (2025)**; tracking ~$1.5–2B contracted for FY2026. Still **loss-making by design** — Anduril projects a **≈$1.2B operating loss in 2026** as it burns to build manufacturing capacity (Arsenal-1) and R&D. This is the deliberate mirror-image of [[entities/palantir]]'s Q2-2026 ≈$1.06B *net income*: Anduril is *buying capacity*, Palantir is *harvesting software margin*.
- **Funding lineage:** ~$30.5B (a year prior) → **$5B Series H at $61B valuation (2026-05-13**, led by Thrive Capital + Andreessen Horowitz) → **in talks at ≈$100B (2026-07-24, >3× a year prior)**. As of **2026-09-18 the ≈$100B round is NOT yet confirmed closed** — reported as a possible two-stage structure (a second tranche at a higher mark), both tranches potentially within the year. A closed $100B mark would exceed **Northrop Grumman (≈$77B public cap)** — a privately-held defense startup worth more than an 87-year-old prime.
- **2026-09-01 — Army TITAN into production:** the Army moved its **Tactical Intelligence Targeting Access Node (TITAN)** to production, splitting the delivery order: **Palantir ≈$127M + Anduril ≈$65M** for **8 systems (4 Advanced + 4 Basic)** over ~18 months, with a further order anticipated in **FY2027**. TITAN is a crewed ground station fusing space/high-altitude/aerial/terrestrial sensors into targeting for long-range precision fires — a concrete dated instance of the *two US primes splitting a single modernization line* rather than competing head-to-head.

## Why it matters (the layer up)

Anduril is the empirical test of the [[concepts/technological-republic]] "commercial prime replaces the government lab" claim in its *hardware* form. Palantir proved a software company could become a defense prime on software margins; Anduril is trying to prove a *venture-funded startup can out-manufacture the legacy primes* — a far more capital-intensive and unproven bet (hence the loss). If the ≈$100B round closes, defense-VC has funded a would-be hardware prime larger than Northrop on the strength of software-defined manufacturing alone — the strongest single datapoint for [[synthesis/techno-industrial-state-defense-tech-six-region|the re-coupling being structural, not cyclical]]. The falsifier is symmetric: a hardware-scaling prime that never reaches profitability, in a procurement system that still favours incumbents, is exactly how a defense-tech bubble would look.
