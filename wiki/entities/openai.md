# OpenAI

US AI company founded 2015 (non-profit) → 2019 "capped-profit" structure → transitioning to for-profit in 2026. Creator of GPT series, ChatGPT, o-series reasoning models, and the OpenAI Agents API. Headquartered in San Francisco.

## Key facts (as of 2026-11-04)

- Funding round trimmed to ~$20B goal after securing ~$11B; pre-money valuation reported at $1.25–1.4 trillion (sources conflict).
- Former US Army Secretary Dan Driscoll hired as senior counselor for national-security modernization (Nov 2, 2026).
- Election Operations Center launched for US midterms; active until all votes counted.
- Abu Dhabi energy and investment agreements signed.
- GPT-6 Rosalind research model reported by aggregators but unverified by primary sources.
- Acquired OpenClaw creator [[entities/peter-steinberger]] as personal-agents lead in February 2026, shortly after OpenClaw went viral.
