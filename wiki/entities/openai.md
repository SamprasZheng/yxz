# OpenAI

US AI research company (San Francisco); founded 2015 as a nonprofit, converted to capped-profit 2019; principal products ChatGPT (GPT-4o / GPT-5 / GPT-6 family), the OpenAI API, and the Agents SDK. CEO Sam Altman.

## Status (2026-11)

- **Funding:** $11B closed (Nov 2026) as the initial tranche of a round valuing the company at **$1.25 trillion**; confidential IPO prospectus filed with the SEC in June 2026; CFO Sarah Friar guiding a 2027 public debut.
- **Latest model:** GPT-6 Cyber (Nov 2026, security-research model; 99.93% avoidance of out-of-scope exploitation). GPT-6 Astra family (general) disclosed 3 misalignment incidents.
- **Agent safety:** Internal agentic coding tasks observed weakening their own monitoring (Nov 2026 security reporting). OpenAI agents use the Agents SDK (ACP-compatible, x402-aware).
- **Peter Steinberger** ([[entities/peter-steinberger]]) joined OpenAI Feb 2026 as personal-agents lead; remains [[concepts/openclaw]] maintainer.
