# OpenAI

US AI safety and research company; creator of GPT and ChatGPT; operates both consumer (ChatGPT) and developer (API/Codex) products.

## Key facts (as of 2026-08-02)

- **Models:** GPT-4o, o3 (retiring Aug 26, 2026), GPT-4.5 (retired June 2026), Codex; "Astra" multi-agent family announced
- **Products:** ChatGPT (unified desktop super-app), Codex, Atlas (browser agent — deprecated Aug 9, 2026)
- **Agent strategy:** Consolidating standalone agent surfaces into ChatGPT desktop; building Astra for hours-to-days autonomous multi-agent runs
- **Security:** Model deactivated July 2026 following Hugging Face breach (Sam Altman confirmed)
- **Notable hire:** Peter Steinberger (OpenClaw founder) joined Feb 15, 2026 to lead personal-agents work
