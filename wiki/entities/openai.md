# OpenAI

US AI company founded 2015; creator of GPT / ChatGPT / o-series reasoning models and the Codex / DALL·E / Sora product lines. Operates as a capped-profit entity controlled by a non-profit board. Primary closed-frontier rival to Anthropic.

Key 2026 milestones: IPO S-1 expected SEC EDGAR filing (Sept 2026); $2B/month revenue; $14B 2026 projected loss; ChatGPT for Teachers US expansion; Brazil and Thailand market entries.

Products relevant to this wiki: OpenAI Agent SDK, Assistants API, Realtime API; ACP (Agentic Commerce Protocol) co-developed with Stripe; GPT-OSS open-weight release.
