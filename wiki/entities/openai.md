# OpenAI

US AI research and deployment company. Creator of the GPT series and ChatGPT. Operates as a capped-profit LLC (converted from non-profit). Sam Altman is CEO; Greg Brockman is President.

## Key products

- **ChatGPT** — consumer LLM product; >1.2 billion users (Altman, DealBook Nov 2026)
- **GPT-6 / GPT-6.2** — flagship model family (GPT-6.2 released ~Nov 2026, Astra pricing cut 20%)
- **OpenAI API** — developer platform
- **OpenAI Agent SDK** — released 2025; part of the US-led open agent-runtime ecosystem

## 2026 context

- Annualized revenue grew +70% Q2→Q3 2026 (DealBook Nov 18)
- California AG sued OpenAI over AI safety failures (80+ rogue-agent incidents) Nov 2026
- Oregon filed $2.035B suit over ChatGPT child-safety failures Nov 2026
- Safety agreement signed with four external evaluator firms (Apollo, others) Nov 2026
- Runs persistent OpenClaw agents on its own codebase (per OpenClaw's disclosure)
- OpenAI is backing [[concepts/openclaw]] Enterprise alongside Red Hat and [[entities/nvidia]]
