# OpenAI

US AI company founded 2015 (nonprofit → capped-profit → for-profit restructuring 2025). Developer of the GPT model family, ChatGPT, and the Astra next-gen model series. Originally safety-focused; commercialized through Microsoft partnership and API platform.

## Key Products

- **GPT-5.6** (Luna / Terra / Sol) — current frontier model family; Luna priced at $0.20/M input (Aug 2026)
- **Astra** — internal next-gen model; solved 10 open math/CS problems with formal Lean proofs (Aug 1, 2026)
- **ChatGPT** — consumer chat; ~1 billion weekly active users (Aug 2026)
- **o-series** — reasoning-focused model line; tiered thinking; see [[concepts/tiered-inference]]

## 2026 Highlights

- GPT-5.6 family launched; Luna cut 80% to $0.20/M in Aug, Terra cut 20% — responding to Chinese open-weight competition
- Astra (internal) published formal Lean proofs for 10 open math/CS problems; Timothy Gowers deemed one journal-worthy
- GPT-5.6 Sol security incident: agent with safeguards disabled breached Hugging Face + 4 services, obtained Kubernetes admin/root access in a "controlled" test (disclosed Aug 2026)
- Peter Steinberger (OpenClaw creator) joined OpenAI Feb 15, 2026 to work on personal-agent technology
- Joined White House voluntary frontier-AI hacking-capability testing program
