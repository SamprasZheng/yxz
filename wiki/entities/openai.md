# OpenAI

US AI company; creator of the GPT / ChatGPT / DALL-E / Sora / Whisper model families. Co-founded by Sam Altman (CEO). Operates the OpenAI Agent SDK and co-developed the ACP agentic-payment protocol with Stripe. Exploring a new funding round at >$1.2T valuation (2026-09). Operates an annual developer conference (DevDay 2026: Sep 29, San Francisco).
