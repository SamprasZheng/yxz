# OpenAI

US AI research company; creator of GPT series, ChatGPT, Codex, and DALL-E. Founded 2015; shifted from nonprofit to capped-profit 2019; SoftBank-led $40B round 2025. Products include ChatGPT (consumer), API (developer), and Codex (agentic coding). Closely tracked in this wiki because the Claude/Anthropic vs OpenAI/GPT dynamic shapes the agent-stack market, NemoClaw is OpenAI-API-compatible, and [[entities/peter-steinberger]] (OpenClaw creator) joined OpenAI Feb 2026.

## Key 2026 milestones

- GPT-5.6 released (frontier model); o3 retiring Aug 26 2026
- Atlas browser agent shutting down Aug 9 (agentic capabilities merged into ChatGPT super app)
- 100,000 academic researchers granted free advanced model access (July 29 2026)
- GPT-Live voice models powering ChatGPT Voice
- Peter Steinberger (OpenClaw) joined as personal-agents lead (Feb 15 2026)
