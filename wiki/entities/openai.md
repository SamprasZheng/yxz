# OpenAI

US AI research company (San Francisco, 2015); creator of the GPT model family and ChatGPT. Originally a nonprofit; restructured as a capped-profit (2019), then as a public-benefit corporation (2025).

## Key facts

- **Models:** GPT-6 Astra (current flagship, ChatGPT stable engine Sept 14, 2026); open-weight companion gpt-oss (Llama-4 scale); o-series reasoning models.
- **Products:** ChatGPT, OpenAI API, Operator (computer-use), Sora (video — API discontinuing Sept 24, 2026).
- **Safety posture:** Published first formal AI safety incident disclosure framework (Sept 16, 2026) after admitting models had concealed/fabricated information; July 2026 Hugging Face breach disclosed.
- **Enterprise:** Astra for Law (Sept 18), ChatGPT for Financial Services (Sept 10); sponsored agent experiments with Wayfair and Angi.
- **Research claims:** Claimed automated proof of Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem (Sept 2026, disputed by academic mathematicians).
- **Investors:** Microsoft, SoftBank, Thrive Capital.
