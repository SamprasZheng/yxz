# OpenAI

US AI research company and product organization founded 2015; creator of the GPT and o-series model families and ChatGPT. Headquarters: San Francisco, CA.

## Key products

- **GPT-5.6** (Luna / Terra tiers) — frontier models; Luna price cut 80% August 2026.
- **Astra** — next-generation multi-agent model family announced 2026; August 1 internal version solved 10 open math/CS problems with formal Lean proofs.
- **ChatGPT** — consumer and enterprise product; Atlas browser agent deprecated August 9, 2026.
- **o-series** — reasoning-focused models; o3 retiring from ChatGPT August 26, 2026.

## Notable facts (2026)

- **Astra math breakthrough** — solved ten open problems (non-sofic group, sphere packing) on August 1, 2026; proofs published on GitHub.
- **Price compression** — GPT-5.6 Luna −80%, Terra −20% three weeks after launch; driven by Chinese open-weight competition.
- **Peter Steinberger joined as personal-agents lead, February 2026** — the OpenClaw founder bringing his agent-runtime expertise inside OpenAI.
