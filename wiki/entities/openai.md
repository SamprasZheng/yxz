# OpenAI

US AI company (San Francisco); founded 2015 as a nonprofit, now a capped-profit structure. Primary developer of the GPT and o-series model families. CEO Sam Altman. Major investors include Microsoft (~49% economic stake), SoftBank, and others; valuation >$300B as of 2026.

## Model Lineage (2026)

- **GPT-6 Astra** (Sept 3, 2026) — flagship; AA Index v4.3 ≈53, tied with Claude Fable 5.1 for closed-frontier lead
- **gpt-oss** — open-weight line (open-as-funnel strategy); Llama 4 equivalent tier
- o-series — reasoning/chain-of-thought models (o3, o4-mini, etc.)

## Developer Platform

- **Agents API** — public beta opened September 10, 2026; enables orchestrated multi-step agent pipelines with tool use, handoffs, and memory
- **OpenAI Agent SDK** — used in [[synthesis/agent-runtime-orchestration-six-region]] US ecosystem
- Financial tool for investment bankers debuted Sept 10, 2026

## Governance

- **Nonprofit board** (Sept 2026): Paul Christiano (AI safety researcher, ex-ARC) named board member Sept 9
- Sam Altman public signal (Sept 11): company "open to slowing cutting-edge AI development" — notable posture statement on pace vs safety
