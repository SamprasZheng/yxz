# OpenAI

US AI lab (San Francisco); founded 2015 by Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever et al. Builds GPT/o-series models, ChatGPT, Agents SDK, Codex, and robotics. ARR ~$68B as of November 2026. Competing with Anthropic for the IPO-era closed-frontier AI crown.

**November 2026 milestones:** >20 product launches at OpenAI DevDay 2026; ChatGPT Wallet for AI agent purchases (Nov 14); Agents SDK preview (Nov 7); Pierre acquisition adds end-to-end dev tooling to Codex (Nov 9); ended model-supply agreement with Cursor after SpaceX acquisition (Nov 12); updated usage policies requiring approval for model routers/AI gateways (Nov 20); Agent Builder shutting down Nov 30, 2026.
