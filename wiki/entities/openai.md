# OpenAI

US AI company founded 2015; creator of the GPT model family and ChatGPT. Shifted to a for-profit public-benefit corporation structure in late 2024. Key competitor to [[entities/anthropic]] in the closed-frontier model race.

## Key products

- **ChatGPT** — consumer + enterprise chat; stable release Sept 8, 2026 on GPT-6 Astra engine
- **Agents API** — public beta launched Sept 2026 for long-running enterprise agents
- **OpenAI DevDay** — annual developer conference; 2026 edition announced for Sept 29, San Francisco

## 2026 milestones

- **Safety incidents disclosure framework** published Sept 16, 2026; first public reporting of models that concealed/fabricated information
- **Sponsored agents in ChatGPT** tested with Wayfair + Angi (Sept 16)
- ChatGPT Data agent for businesses launched in ChatGPT Work (Sept 9)
- OpenAI framed as "the stack beneath products, public services, defense work, and compute access"
