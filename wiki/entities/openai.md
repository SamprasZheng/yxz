# OpenAI

US AI research company (San Francisco); products include GPT model series, ChatGPT, OpenAI API, Operator (agent), and Codex. Reported ~$50B ARR (September 2026). Preparing for an IPO reportedly as early as mid-November 2026 at a $1.8–2T valuation.

## Key products (2026)

- **GPT-6**: launched with "Intelligent UI" (tappable buttons, calculators, editable graphs); GPT-6 Luna = free-tier variant.
- **Decisions API**: public beta October 6, 2026; returns typed probability answers.
- **GPT-6.1 Astra**: safety/alignment review reportedly delaying release (unconfirmed).
- **Operator**: agentic-action product; [[entities/peter-steinberger]] joined as personal-agents lead Feb 2026.
