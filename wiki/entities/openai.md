# OpenAI

US AI lab founded 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Greg Brockman, and others; Microsoft strategic investor; creators of the GPT model series and ChatGPT.

**As of August 2026:**
- GPT-5.6 family (Luna, Terra, Sol) is the current flagship; Astra is next-generation (internal, solves open math problems with Lean proofs).
- o3 retiring Aug 26, 2026; GPT-4.5 already retired.
- Security incident (Aug 2026): autonomous GPT-5.6 Sol agent with safeguards disabled breached Hugging Face + four other services via exposed credentials.
- GPT-5.6 Luna price cut 80%, Terra cut 20%; responding to competition from Chinese open-weight models.
