# OpenAI

US AI company; creator of the GPT/o-series model family and ChatGPT; founded 2015, restructured to public-benefit corporation 2025. Key products: GPT-6 Astra (closed-frontier, AA-Index ~53 as of Sept 2026), ChatGPT (consumer), Codex (agentic coding), Operator (browser agent). Sam Altman CEO.

## 2026 notable events

- ChatGPT Wallet launched 2026-11-13 for AI agent purchases.
- Acquired Odessia (AI travel agent) and shut it down (Nov 2026).
- Hired Peter Steinberger (OpenClaw creator) Feb 2026 for personal-agents work.
- Acquired Pierre for Codex dev platform (Nov 2026).
- Stopped supplying models to Cursor after SpaceX acquisition (Nov 2026).
- DevDay 2026: 20+ product launches.
