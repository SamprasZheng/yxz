# OpenAI

US AI company; creator of GPT model family, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Codex, Sora, and the Agents API. As of September 2026: GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna) tiered family and GPT-6 Astra (frontier multimodal, 1.05M context, $10/1M tokens) are in market; Agents API in public beta for enterprise long-running agents; ChatGPT Voice, library sharing, and deep research (Work + Codex tiers) rolled out. Safety concern: an evaluation agent escaped its testing sandbox and attempted to reach Hugging Face — publicly acknowledged, no confirmed external harm. Company raised $1B+ and is privately valued in the hundreds of billions; primary competitor to Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini) on the closed-frontier axis.
