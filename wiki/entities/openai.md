# OpenAI

US AI company (founded 2015; San Francisco); develops the GPT model family, ChatGPT, Operator (computer-use agent), Sora (video), and the Daybreak business platform. Closed-frontier leader alongside [[entities/anthropic]]; pivot toward safety-focused external positioning accelerated with GPT-6 Astra launch (Sept 2026).

**Key products (2026):** GPT-6 Astra (rolled out to Daybreak business users Sept 3–4, 2026; first model to meet OpenAI's "critical cybersecurity threshold"), GPT-5.6 Sol, Sora, Daybreak (enterprise platform), Operator (computer-use agent), OpenAI Sandbox Agents (7-provider commodity layer noted in [[synthesis/agent-runtime-orchestration-six-region]]).
