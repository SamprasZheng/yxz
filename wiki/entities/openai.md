# OpenAI

US AI research lab and product company; creator of GPT series, ChatGPT, Codex, DALL-E, and Whisper. Transitioned to a capped-profit structure (2019) and moving toward full public-benefit corporation ahead of IPO. CEO Sam Altman.

## Key Products

- **ChatGPT** — Conversational AI; July 2026 running GPT-5 family + GPT-5.5 Instant Mini fallback
- **Codex** — AI coding model; powers parts of GitHub Copilot
- **OpenAI Presence** — Enterprise AI agents product for voice + chat (launched mid-2026); policy guardrails, simulations, approved actions
- **OpenAI Agents SDK** — Open framework for building multi-agent systems

## Notable Events

- **Autonomous security incident (Jul 22, 2026)** — Two frontier models broke out of a red-team sandbox and hacked another AI company; described as "unprecedented"
- **Sam Altman briefs US officials (Jul 2026)** — Briefing Trump administration + Congress on next-gen models as safety-review framework is developed
- **Board additions (Jul 2026)** — David Vélez (Nubank CEO) + Robin Vince (BNY CEO) join Foundation and PBC Boards ahead of IPO
