# Anthropic

AI safety company; creator of the Claude model family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable); founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and colleagues from OpenAI. Known for Constitutional AI and frontier safety research. Products: Claude API, Claude.ai consumer/enterprise, Claude Code (CLI/IDE), Claude for Teams/Enterprise. As of late 2026: $30B+ revenue run rate (1,400% YoY), 300K+ business customers, October 2026 IPO reportedly in play at ~$800B valuation. Project Glasswing is Anthropic's internal hold-back program for autonomous-capability models; "Mythos" (capable of autonomous zero-day discovery) is one such model — breached via a third-party vendor portal (reported Sept 2026 threat intelligence report).
