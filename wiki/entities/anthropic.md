# Anthropic

US AI safety company (San Francisco); founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and former OpenAI colleagues. Primary developer of the Claude model family (Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku lineages). Significant investor presence from Google, Spark Capital, and others; estimated valuation >$60B as of 2026.

## Claude Model Lineage (2026)

- **Claude Fable 5.1** (Sept 1, 2026) — flagship capability model, AA Index v4.3 score ≈53, tied with GPT-6 Astra for closed-frontier lead
- **Claude Mythos 5.1** (Sept 1, 2026) — identical capability to Fable 5.1 with reduced safeguards for coding, scientific research, and knowledge work
- Earlier: Opus 5, Sonnet 5, Haiku 4.5 family (H1 2026)

## Developer Platform

- **Claude Code** — CLI/IDE/web/desktop agent for software engineering; self-hosted environments in public beta for Team and Enterprise (Sept 2026); inference hooks for security-server inspection in beta
- **Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS)** (Sept 2026) — zero data retention + customer-cloud-hosted misuse monitoring
- **Claude Agent SDK** — multi-agent orchestration; MCP-native; used in [[entities/nvidia]] NemoClaw + [[concepts/hermes-agent-framework]] integrations
