# Anthropic

US AI safety company; maker of the Claude LLM family and Claude Code.

## Key facts (as of October 2026)

- Founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and former OpenAI colleagues.
- Products: Claude model family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable tiers), Claude Code CLI, Claude Agent SDK, Claude API.
- **May 2026 Series H:** $65B at ~$965B post-money valuation.
- **June 2026:** Confidential S-1 filed with SEC; IPO marketing expected mid-October 2026 at the earliest (pre-US-midterm target).
- Annualized revenue run rate ~$47B by May 2026 (up from ~$9B end-2025).
- Claude Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 / Sonnet 5.5 are the September 2026 frontier releases; see [[synthesis/open-weight-llm-agent-stack-six-region]] for benchmarking context.
