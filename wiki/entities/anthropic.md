# Anthropic

US AI safety company; creator of the Claude model family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable) and Claude Code agentic CLI. Founded 2021 by Dario Amodei (CEO), Daniela Amodei (President), and colleagues from OpenAI.

## Key Products

- **Claude models** — Haiku (fastest/cheapest), Sonnet (balanced), Opus (flagship reasoning), Fable (narrative/creative)
- **Claude Opus 5** — Launched 2026-07-24; fastest, most cost-efficient flagship; default on Claude Max/Pro
- **Claude Code** — Terminal-based agentic coding assistant; July 2026 added Government Desktop (FedRAMP High) and built-in browser
- **Claude Cowork** — Collaborative AI workspace

## Notable

- Economic Futures Research Fund (Jul 2026) — studying AI's economic impact
- $20M donation to Public First Action (Jul 2026)
- AI for Science rare-disease research grants (Jul 2026)
- $10M commitment to Canadian AI research (Jul 2026)
