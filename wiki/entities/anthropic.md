# Anthropic

US AI safety company (San Francisco); founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and former OpenAI team. Builds the Claude model family (Opus/Sonnet/Haiku/Fable/Mythos series) and Claude Code agentic IDE/SDK. Largest closed-frontier AI lab by safety research output.

**November 2026 milestones:** Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 launched Nov 9 (30% faster than 5.1); Mythos 5.2 restricted to vetted orgs (Life Sciences + Cyber Verification Programs). IPO targeted week of Nov 16, aiming to raise at ~$100B valuation on $33.24B revenue (9 months to Sep 30, 2026); Q3 operating loss $567M. Claude Managed Agents + Claude for Manufacturing shipped Nov 12; linked billing on Platform added Nov 17.
