# Anthropic

US AI safety company founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and former OpenAI researchers. Builders of the Claude model family (Haiku → Sonnet → Opus → Fable → Mythos tiers). Mission: responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity.

## Key products (2026)

- **Claude model family** — Claude 4 → 5 series; Fable 5.1 + Mythos 5.1 released 2026-09-01 with differentiated safeguard levels
- **Claude Code** — CLI + IDE agentic coding assistant; Sept 2026 update added fullscreen diff panel, policy diagnostics
- **Claude desktop** — built-in browser side panel rolling out Sept 2026
- **Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS)** — zero data retention + customer-hosted misuse detection; announced Sept 2026

## Notable milestones

- IPO prospectus expected after US Labor Day 2026; investor day mid-September; planned US exchange listing
- Amazon and Google as anchor cloud partners
- Claude for Teachers pilot in Detroit Public Schools (fall 2026)
