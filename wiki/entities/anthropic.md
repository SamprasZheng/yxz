# Anthropic

US AI safety company; maker of the Claude family of models. Co-founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and others from OpenAI.

## Key Products (as of 2026)

- **Claude models** — Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable / Mythos tiers; Fable 5.1 + Mythos 5.1 launched Sept 1 2026
- **Claude Code** — AI-native developer CLI and SDK; self-hosted environments in public beta (Sept 2026)
- **Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS)** — Launched Sept 1 2026; inference hooks for enterprise security inspection
- **Mythos 5.1** — Gated to US Cyber Verification Program (CVP) and Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP); a capability tier insulated from open-weight competition

## Business Status (2026)

- Series H-1 private valuation ~$965B (May 2026)
- $15B pre-IPO credit facility finalizing
- $2T IPO target: October 2026
- Reported ~$1.25B/month compute demand (cited in [[synthesis/orbital-data-center-six-region]])
