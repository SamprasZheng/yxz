# Anthropic

US AI safety company founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and other ex-OpenAI researchers. Developer of the Claude model family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Mythos / Fable) and Claude Code CLI. Mission-focused on safe, steerable AI; known for Constitutional AI and model-card transparency.

## Key Products

- **Claude** — closed-frontier LLM family; Claude Opus 5 (~60.7 on Arena; see [[synthesis/open-weight-llm-agent-stack-six-region]])
- **Claude Code** — agentic CLI and IDE extension for software development; see [[concepts/hermes-agent-framework]] for the agent-runtime context
- **Claude.ai / Cowork** — consumer and team chat surfaces

## 2026 Highlights

- Claude Sonnet 5 launched with promotional pricing ($2/$10/M); standard ($3/$15) from Sep 1, 2026
- $10B compute deal signed Aug 3, 2026; major infrastructure expansion
- Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar joined as Chief Global Affairs Officer Aug 4, 2026
- Inference hooks launched in Enterprise beta for real-time DLP across chat, Claude Code, and Cowork
- Joined White House voluntary frontier-AI hacking-capability testing program (alongside OpenAI/Google/Meta)
