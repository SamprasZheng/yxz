# Anthropic

US AI safety company founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and former OpenAI colleagues. Creator of the Claude model family (Claude 1 → 3 → 4 → 5 series); builder of [[concepts/nemoclaw]]-adjacent tooling via Claude Code. Headquartered in San Francisco.

## Key facts (as of 2026-11-04)

- Q3-2026 revenue ~$17B, up from ~$1.4B a year earlier; >$895B in committed spending disclosed in IPO filing.
- IPO expected November 2026 on Nasdaq; May 2026 Series H valued company at ~$965B.
- SEC S-1/A filing warns Anthropic may be near its own recursive self-improvement (RSI) threshold and is reviewing its Responsible Scaling Policy.
- 15-person chip team already uses Claude for engineering and design work (disclosed to IPO investors).
- Claude Code Opus 5.5 is the flagship agentic coding model; version cadence 2.1.309–2.1.317 in November 2026.
- Expanded AI for Science program: free Claude credits for 20 research institutions.
