# Anthropic

US AI safety company founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and former OpenAI researchers. Developer of the Claude model family and Claude Code.

## Key facts

- **Models (Nov 2026):** Fable 5.2 (GA), Mythos 5.2 (restricted — Life Sciences + Cyber Verification Programs), Sonnet 4.6 (current Claude Code default), Sonnet 4.5 retiring Nov 30 2026.
- **Claude Code:** Agentic developer tool; v2.1.317-318 shipped Nov 2-3 2026; security incident (2,539 secrets exposed on GitHub, Nov 9) acknowledged by Claude Code lead.
- **IPO (expected Nov 16 week, 2026):** Targeting up to $100B raise, which would be the largest IPO on record; $33.24B revenue nine months ended Sep 30, 2026.
- **Infrastructure:** $3.4B spent acquiring queue position for 1.7 GW Caterpillar gas generators for data centers (from OnePWR Solutions).
- **Revenue trajectory:** Claude Code exceeded $1B run-rate revenue ~6 months post-GA (announced Nov 2025).
