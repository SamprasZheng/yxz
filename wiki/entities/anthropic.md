# Anthropic

US AI safety company (San Francisco, 2021); founded by former OpenAI leadership including Dario Amodei (CEO) and Daniela Amodei (President). Creator of the Claude model family.

## Key facts

- **Models:** Claude family — most recent Claude Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1 (Sept 1, 2026); AA Index v4.3 score = 53, tied for closed-frontier lead with GPT-6 Astra.
- **Revenue:** Annualized revenue projected to exceed $100B in 2026 (Bloomberg/NYT, 2026-09-18); IPO targeting as early as November 2026.
- **Products:** Claude (consumer + API), Claude Code (agentic coding CLI), Cowork (team collaboration), inference hooks (beta Sept 2026).
- **Investors:** Google, Amazon, Spark Capital.
- **Safety focus:** Published monthly threat intelligence reports; Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP); Constitutional AI.
