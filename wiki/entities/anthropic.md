# Anthropic

US AI safety company founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and others; creator of the Claude model family and Claude Code.

**As of August 2026:**
- Claude Opus 5 is the current flagship (faster, more cost-efficient coding / knowledge / science model).
- Claude Code (CLI + IDE) is the primary agentic coding product; v2.1.221 released Aug 3 with VSCode Focus view, credential masking, background `/code-review` subagent.
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) surpassed 400M monthly SDK downloads in 2026 (4× YoY); 2026-07-28 spec includes stronger authorization and versioned extensions.
- Enterprise partnerships: Cognizant expanded Jul 27, 2026.
- Joined White House AI safety meeting Aug 3, 2026 alongside OpenAI and Google.
