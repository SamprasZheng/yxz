# Anthropic

US AI safety company; creator of the Claude model family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable / Mythos lines). Founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and colleagues from OpenAI. Operators of Claude Code, Claude Design, Claude Docs, and Claude Slides. Targeting a $2T IPO in October 2026.

## Model family (as of 2026-09)
- Claude Haiku 4.5 — fast lightweight tier
- Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Sep 28 2026) — 30% faster, 30% cheaper than prior Sonnet
- Claude Opus 5.5 (Sep 22 2026) — matches Fable 5.1 on most work, 40% cheaper than Opus 5
- Claude Fable 5.1 (Sep 1 2026) — frontier closed model; AA benchmark v4.3 score ≈53
- Claude Mythos 5.1 (Sep 1 2026) — new tier announced alongside Fable 5.1
