# Anthropic

US AI safety company; creator of the Claude model family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable / Mythos lines). Co-founded 2021 by Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei (ex-OpenAI). Headquartered in San Francisco.

## Status (as of 2026-11-10)

- **IPO imminent**: formal marketing can begin 2026-11-10 after waiting period; Nasdaq debut expected week of Nov 16; valuation expectations $1.1–1.3T (down from $1.5–2T earlier in Nov).
- **Fable 5.2** unveiled 2026-11-09: targets harder software engineering tasks, faster and cheaper than predecessor; Mythos model family also updated.
- **Infrastructure**: $3.4B agreement for gas-fired generators for AI data centers (FT/Bloomberg, 2026-11-08).
- **Claude Code security**: Apiiro (2026-11-08) reported 1,122 public GitHub repos leaking secrets via the "don't ask again" project settings file; no Anthropic public response as of that date.
