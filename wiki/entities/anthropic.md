# Anthropic

US AI safety company founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and others from OpenAI; creator of the [[concepts/nemotron|Claude]] family of models. Raised $7.3B+ including $4B from Amazon (2023–2024) and $3B from Google. Principal backer of [[entities/sampras|Spacesharks]] stack indirectly through Claude Code and Claude APIs.

## Key products

- **Claude** — flagship LLM family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable / Mythos tiers)
- **Claude Code** — agentic coding CLI + SDK; powers this wiki's automated agents
- **Claude Enterprise / Cowork** — enterprise deployment with inference hooks and smart reports

## 2026 milestones

- **Claude Fable 5.1** released 2026-09-01; **Claude Mythos 5.1** same period
- **Claude for Small Business** launched 2026-09-15: 43 workflows + 27 connectors
- Claude now leads 26% of Anthropic's own model R&D (announced 2026-09-17)
- First operating profit expected Q3 2026
