# Anthropic

US AI safety company founded 2021 by former OpenAI researchers (Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and others). Builds the Claude model family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus / Fable tiers) and Claude Code agentic development tool. Headquartered in San Francisco.

## Key facts

- Primary model line: Claude (Haiku 4.5 / Sonnet / Opus 5 / Fable 5 as of 2026)
- Claude Code: agentic CLI + SDK; auto mode made default Aug 14, 2026 (Pro/Max/Team)
- Aug 2026: negotiating acquisition of Decart AI (~$6B, real-time generative video / world models); signed $9B compute deal (Riot Platforms) + $10B compute deal (unnamed cloud startup)
- Preparing for possible public-market debut, fall 2026
- Opus 5 priced at ~half of Fable 5 in response to Chinese AI pricing competition
