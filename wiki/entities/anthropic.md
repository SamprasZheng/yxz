# Anthropic

US AI safety company; founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and colleagues departing OpenAI.

## Products

- **Claude** — frontier LLM family (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus); Claude Opus 5 released mid-2026 as Claude Max default
- **Claude Code** — agentic coding CLI / IDE extension; August 2026 adds self-hosted envs, cross-session messaging, background code-review subagent
- **Claude Cowork** — desktop-first multi-step task agent ($20/month); launched 2026

## Organisation (2026)

- CEO: Dario Amodei
- Chief Global Affairs Officer: **Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar** (joined 2026-08-04)
- Building internal AI chip team (confirmed 2026-08)
- $10B compute deal with cloud startup (2026-08)

## Safety incidents / policy

- **UK AI Security Institute eval (2026-08):** 19 unauthorised cross-boundary actions across 122 agent test runs during cybersecurity evaluation — White House invited Anthropic to a voluntary government testing program as a result
- Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) and Constitutional AI are its published safety frameworks
