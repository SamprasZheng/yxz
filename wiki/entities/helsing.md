# Helsing

German defense-AI company — the **European public-private-fusion archetype** in [[synthesis/techno-industrial-state-defense-tech-six-region]], the sovereign-European counterpart to the US [[entities/palantir]]/Anduril primes. Builds AI software and autonomous systems for the air, land, sea, and sub-surface domains, sold to European armed forces. Positioned explicitly as *"the sovereign-European alternative to Anduril"* and, by mid-2026, **Germany's most valuable startup**.

## Key facts

- **Founded March 2021**, headquartered **Munich**; subsidiaries in the **UK, France, and Estonia** (deliberately multi-sovereign — the structural answer to Europe's "fractured across 27 sovereignties" soft-belief problem in [[concepts/soft-belief]]).
- **Founders:** Torsten Reil (games/AI, ex-NaturalMotion), Gundbert Scherf (ex-German Ministry of Defence), Niklas Köhler (machine-learning engineer).
- Roughly **~80% European-held** ownership — the sovereignty claim is in the cap table, not just the marketing.

## Product portfolio (the layer down — actual systems, not vibes)

| System | Domain | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Altra** | Land / ISR | AI software fusing reconnaissance data for land combat — the original flagship |
| **HX-2 / HF-1** | Air | AI loitering munitions; navigate and target **without GPS** (EW-contested / GPS-denied environments) |
| **Centaur** | Air | AI combat co-pilot; **flew live combat scenarios in Saab's Gripen E** fighter |
| **Lura** | Sub-surface | Persistent **underwater surveillance** AI (unveiled May 2025); autonomous subsurface-threat detection, incl. SG-1 Fathom gliders |
| **CA-1 Europa** | Air | Unmanned-fighter / collaborative-combat-aircraft ("wingman") concept |

The GPS-denied targeting in HX-2 is the concrete link to the electronic-warfare / contested-RF theme running through the RF cluster ([[synthesis/phased-array-rf-frontend-supply-chain]]) — autonomy is the hedge against a jammed spectrum.

## Funding lineage (fact-checked 2026-08-27)

The valuation trajectory is itself the [[synthesis/techno-industrial-state-defense-tech-six-region#falsifier-table|"defense-tech re-coupling is structural"]] falsifier at the European scale:

- **Series D (June 2025):** ≈€12B (~$14B) valuation, led by Prima Materia (Daniel Ek / Spotify) and General Catalyst; earlier backers include Lightspeed, Accel.
- **Series E — CLOSED 2026-07-13:** **$1.8B raised at an $18B valuation** — **Europe's biggest-ever defense-technology startup round**; oversubscribed ("investor demand significantly exceeded allocation"). Lead **Dragoneer Investment Group**; participants **Iconiq, Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPPIB), JPMorgan Chase, and Goldman Sachs Alternatives**. Funds AI software, autonomous strike drones, underwater surveillance, and next-generation platforms for European militaries.

## Why it matters (the layer up — the European sovereignty bet)

Helsing is the test of whether Europe can build a defense-tech prime *at scale* without buying the capability from a US prime. Two tensions define its position within the six-region frame:

- **Sovereign-autonomy pull:** the EU's strategic-autonomy doctrine and the post-2022 rearmament wave (Germany's Zeitenwende, the ~€800B "ReArm Europe"/readiness-2030 envelope) give Helsing a policy tailwind no US startup enjoys — buying European is a stated procurement goal, not just a preference.
- **Fragmentation drag:** its own multi-country subsidiary structure is a workaround for the fact that "European defense procurement" is really 27 national procurements. The [[synthesis/techno-industrial-state-defense-tech-six-region|scorecard]] rates Europe ★★★☆☆ precisely because coordination — not capital or talent — is the binding constraint, and the live counter-signal is that **Germany chose Palantir's MSS** (and NATO went sole-source Palantir in Mar 2025) even as it funds a domestic alternative. Helsing scaling vs. US primes capturing European procurement first is the open question.
