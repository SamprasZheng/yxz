# Palantir Technologies

US defense and intelligence data analytics company. Co-founded by [[entities/alexander-karp]], Peter Thiel, and others (~2003). Core business: software platforms (Gotham, Foundry, AIP) for government intelligence, military operations, and commercial data analysis.

## Key facts

- Early investors/founders: Peter Thiel, Alex Karp, Joe Lonsdale, Stephen Cohen, Nathan Gettings
- Primary clients historically: US intelligence community (CIA, NSA), military, law enforcement
- Commercial platform (Foundry) expanded 2010s–2020s
- AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) launched 2023 — integrates LLMs into enterprise and government workflows; the AIP "bootcamp" GTM motion drove the 2024–2025 commercial-revenue surge
- Listed on NYSE (PLTR)

## Scale (FY2025 + H1 2026, fact-checked 2026-08-07)

The abstract "Western-values defense-tech company" of 2023 became, by 2025–26, one of the most valuable software companies on Earth — the empirical backdrop that makes [[concepts/technological-republic]] more than a manifesto:

- **FY2025 revenue ≈ $4.475B**, +56% YoY (Q4 2025 alone +70% to ≈$1.41B) — verified vs Palantir 8-K earnings releases / Macrotrends
- **Q1 2026 (reported 2026-05-04):** revenue **$1.63B, +85% YoY** — the *fastest growth since the 2020 direct listing* at the time, accelerating vs FY25's +56%; **US revenue $1.28B, +104% YoY** (first time US growth crossed 100%), split US-gov $687M (+84%) and US-commercial $595M (+133%); net income **$870.5M / $0.34 diluted EPS** (vs $214M / $0.08 a year prior, ~4×). FY2026 guidance raised to $7.65–7.66B.
- **Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-03) — growth accelerated *again*:** revenue **$1.935B, +93% YoY** (beat the ~$1.80B consensus by ≈$123M, up from Q1's +85% — a new post-2020-listing high and another quarter of *accelerating* growth); net income **≈$1.06B / $0.41 diluted EPS** (vs $326.7M / $0.13 a year prior, ~3.2×); adjusted EPS **$0.41 vs ~$0.34 est** (~19% surprise). **Total US revenue $1.57B, +115% YoY**, split **US-commercial $764M (+149% YoY)** and **US-gov $809M (+90% YoY)**. FY2026 guidance **raised again to ≈$8.15B** (from the $7.65B set in May); full-year **US-commercial target lifted to >$3.42B (≥134% growth)**. US-commercial — the AIP-bootcamp motion — is now the faster-growing half, out-pacing the historically dominant US-gov line.
- **The de-rating nuance — now partly *reversed*:** in H1 2026 the stock **fell ~27%** (≈$177.75 start-of-year → ≈$129.30 on 2026-07-02) *even as* revenue accelerated — a textbook *multiple-compression-into-accelerating-fundamentals* episode; market cap peaked ≈$424B (Dec 2025), troughed with the sell-off, then re-crossed **$300B in early July**. The Q2 print **snapped that back**: shares jumped **≈+29.5% to $162.66 on 2026-08-04**, trading ≈$155.92 on 2026-08-07 with market cap back to **≈$409B** — recovering most of the way toward the Dec-2025 peak. So the earlier "the *business* re-coupling is realer than the *stock* re-rating" caveat has resolved in the business's favour: the multiple re-expanded once growth *out-ran* the prior compression. The [[concepts/technological-republic]] "priced like a hyperscaler" read survives — the market re-rated back to it after a two-quarter lag. (Note the residual conflict in *per-share YTD* figures across data vendors — some report ≈−12%, some ≈+26% off differing split-adjusted bases; market cap is the cleaner cross-vendor anchor and it is unambiguously near its all-time peak.)
- **Maven Smart System (MSS):** US DoD raised the contract ceiling to **≈$1.3B through 2029** (May 2025); **NATO** signed a **sole-source** procurement of MSS NATO (Mar 2025, ~6-month cycle — among the fastest in NATO history). MSS is the production descendant of Project Maven, the 2017 DoD computer-vision program at the centre of Google's 2018 "Maven" employee revolt — the exact Silicon-Valley-refuses-defense episode Karp built a career criticising.

- **Late-August 2026 continuation (fact-checked 2026-08-27):** the post-Q2 re-rating held and extended — PLTR returned **≈+34% over the three months to late-August** (vs ≈+2% for the S&P 500), and by 2026-08-23 traded at a **forward P/E ≈112×** against a software-sector median ≈23× — i.e. the market is again pricing PLTR like a hyperscaler, the [[concepts/technological-republic]] "priced-for-fusion" read intact. FY2026 guidance reaffirmed at ≈$8.15–8.16B. (Note: an August "Palantir loses $875M contract" headline circulated; the ≈$876M *DCGS-A Increment 1 CD1* IDIQ it referenced is a **2018** Palantir+Raytheon award, not a 2026 event — not added as a current datapoint. A separately reported "lost French DGSI contract" was **disputed by Palantir**, which says the contract was renewed in late 2025 — flagged, unverified.)

## Ideological positioning

Palantir is explicitly a Western-values-aligned defense-tech company. [[entities/alexander-karp]] and Peter Thiel have been vocal that building tools for Western governments is a moral obligation, not just a business:
- Palantir declined to work with certain foreign governments
- Karp publicly criticized Silicon Valley peers for refusing US defense contracts (Google's Maven controversy)

This philosophy is formalized in [[sources/technological-republic-karp-2025]].

## Relevance

The Technological Republic can be read partly as a philosophical rationale for Palantir's business model — critics note the self-serving element; defenders argue the thesis stands independently.
