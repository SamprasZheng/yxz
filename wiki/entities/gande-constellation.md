# Gande Constellation (甘德星座)

China's first **commercial space-based space-debris-monitoring constellation** — a planned **120-satellite** network built by **ATmoto Technology** (also romanised *Aotian Technology* / 奧天科技; Beijing) to detect and track resident space objects across **low, medium and high Earth orbit, 24/7**. It is the China node of the 2026 "SSA sensors go to orbit" structural shift mapped in [[synthesis/space-situational-awareness-six-region]], and a concrete instance of the **"commercial-as-sovereignty"** pattern that [[synthesis/commercial-space-traffic-management-six-region]] identifies for Chinese space-safety capability — a nominally commercial build-out that extends a sovereign, **data-closed** catalog posture (the inverse of the open [[entities/space-track-19sds|Space-Track]] model).

## What it is

- **Operator / builder:** ATmoto / Aotian Technology (奧天科技), Beijing. Chief engineer **Fan Wei** (per Xinhua).
- **Scale & architecture:** **120 satellites**, deployed in **three phases**, full deployment targeted **before 2030**:
  1. **Phase 1 (LX630):** ~**14 satellites** — the baseline detection layer.
  2. **Phase 2:** ~**+36 satellites** adding **high-resolution imaging + electromagnetic (RF) sensors**.
  3. **Phase 3:** ~**+70 AI-equipped satellites** — on-board autonomous detection/triage.
- **Coverage claim:** all-orbit (LEO/MEO/GEO) continuous 24/7 object detection — a space-based vantage that cross-cuts the day/night and weather limits binding ground optical networks (GEODSS, Japan's Bisei, Korea's OWL-Net).

## Deployment status (as of 2026-11)

- **First launch — 2026-07-24** on a **Lijian-1 (ZK-1A)** solid rocket from the Dongfeng commercial space zone, northwest China. Xinhua framed it as "filling a gap in China's commercial space-based debris-monitoring capability." **Per-launch count resolved (fact-check 2026-11-11):** the launch carried **five satellites** into their planned orbits (China Daily HK, 2026-07-26, corroborated by People's Daily / Xinhua English and other outlets) — so the "five satellites" caption was *correct*, and the "Gande-1 01" label refers to the lead spacecraft / first series designation, **not** a single-satellite launch. Read it as a **5-satellite first batch** within the 14-satellite Phase 1.
- **Early capability demonstration:** the first batch **tracked a SpaceX Falcon-9 upper-stage lunar impact on 2026-08-05**, later corroborated spectrally by overseas observatories (per CGTN) — a concrete all-orbit-detection proof point rather than a deployment announcement alone.
- **No second launch reported** as of 2026-11-11 (the remaining ~9 of the 14 Phase-1 satellites not yet flown).

> **Source caveats:** the developer's name appears as *ATmoto*, *AT Moto*, and *Aotian Technology* across outlets. *(The earlier per-launch count discrepancy — "five satellites" vs a single Gande-1 01 — is resolved above: the 2026-07-24 launch deployed five.)*

## Why it matters (cross-domain)

- **Catalog authority as sovereignty.** Like the APOSOS/APSCO optical network under [[concepts/conjunction-screening-providers|conjunction-screening providers]], Gande data is expected to feed a **Chinese sovereign catalog, not an open public feed** — so it widens China's SSA capability without changing its closed-data posture. It does **not** falsify the "China's catalog is sovereign/closed" claim in the six-region map; it reinforces it on a new (space-based) tier.
- **The space-based competition axis.** Gande is the commercial-channel counterpart to the US sovereign-national move — the **SĀCHI** GEO-SDA payload flown on Japan's QZS-6 (2025-02-02) and QZS-7 (2026-08-10) under the now-complete QZSS-HP program ([[entities/18-sds]], [[entities/space-track-19sds]]). Both poles of the governance split moved sensors to orbit in mid-2026.
- **Compute coupling.** A 70-satellite AI-equipped Phase 3 ties Gande to the on-orbit-compute gradient in [[synthesis/orbital-data-center-six-region]] — space-based *sensing* plus on-orbit *triage* is the same edge tier Taiwan sits below ([[synthesis/leo-taiwan-odc-gap]]).
