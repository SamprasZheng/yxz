# Phased-Array RF Front-End — Six-Region Supply-Chain Map

**Question this answers:** Across the phased-array / AESA RF front-end stack (beamformer ICs → GaN power amplifiers → packaging/module → array integration → space-grade transmitter), who leads, who lags, and where does Taiwan actually sit? This is the component-level companion to [[synthesis/leo-taiwan-odc-gap]], which makes the same "strong upstream, absent midstream" argument at the system level.

## The stack, layer by layer

A modern [[concepts/aesa|AESA]] / [[concepts/hybrid-phased-array|hybrid phased array]] is five stacked layers. Value and defensibility differ sharply by layer:

| Layer | What it is | Dominant process | Margin / moat character |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Beamformer IC | Multi-channel phase + gain control (4–8+ ch) | **SiGe BiCMOS / CMOS** | Design-IP heavy; few players; high margin |
| 2. Power amplifier | T/R front-end power | **GaN-on-SiC** (high-power/thermal) **+ GaN-on-Si** (cost-volume) > GaAs > LDMOS | Foundry + thermal; capital heavy (substrate split → *§ below*) |
| 3. Packaging / module | Antenna-in-package, thermal, filters | Substrate / AiP | Integration know-how; mid margin |
| 4. Array integration | Calibration, beam management, [[concepts/dpd-digital-predistortion|DPD]], [[sources/hsieh-xband-leo-transmitter-2020|BIST]] | System | Systems integrator; program-driven |
| 5. Space-grade transmitter | Radiation + vibration qualified ([[concepts/tid-total-ionizing-dose|TID]]/[[concepts/see-single-event-effects|SEE]]) | Qualification | Certification is the hidden gate |

The economic punchline (developed on [[concepts/hybrid-phased-array]]): the *same block diagram* is a $100M defense radar or a sub-$1k consumer SATCOM terminal — the **process mix and integration**, not the architecture, set the price. The commercial inflection of the 2020s is silicon (Layer 1) making flat-panel SATCOM economic. Market sizing for that SATCOM beamformer-IC layer alone (Semiconductor Insight, re-verified 2026-08-30, unchanged): ≈ **USD 2.87B (2025) → 3.21B (2026) → 7.94B (2034)**, **10.6% CAGR**. Broader phased-array beamforming-IC estimates (all applications) span USD ~3.8–6.9B (2025) at 8.6–18.9% CAGR — treat the *direction* (double-digit growth driven by LEO flat-panel volume) as robust and the *level* as source-dependent.

## 2026 structural update — US vendor consolidation at Layers 1–2 (verified 2026-08-30; Skyworks–Qorvo close + OMMIC/MESC correction + MACOM FY2026 added 2026-11-13)

The single biggest change to this map since the 2026-05 build is **US supplier consolidation**, not a shift in national leadership. Three 2020s M&A events re-drew the US column — and the third closed in 2026-10:

- **Layer 2 (GaN PA): Wolfspeed exited RF; [[entities/macom|MACOM]] now owns the merchant GaN-on-SiC RF line.** Wolfspeed sold its entire RF (GaN-on-SiC MMIC) business to MACOM — agreed 2023-08-22, **completed 2023-12-02** — and MACOM **completed the transfer of the Research Triangle Park, NC 100 mm GaN-on-SiC wafer fab on 2025-07-25**, taking full operational control. Wolfspeed then refocused on SiC substrates + power devices and went through Chapter 11 (filed 2025-06-30, **emerged 2025-09-29** with ~70% debt reduction, maturities to 2030). So the pre-2024 "Wolfspeed = US GaN RF-PA leader" framing is **stale** — that node is now **MACOM**, whose **FY2026 revenue reached US$1.33B, +37% YoY** (Q4 FY2026, ended ~2026-10-03: US$424.8M, +62.7% YoY; adj. operating margin 38.2%; Industrial-&-Defense US$164.6M; FY2026 telecom +~11% "driven mainly by 5G and SATCOM"; reported 2026-11-05) — shipping 25 W Ka-band SATCOM GaN PAs 27–31 GHz and 125 W X-band radar GaN MMICs, alongside Qorvo's captive GaN.
- **Layer 1 (beamformer IC): Qorvo absorbed Anokiwave (2024).** Qorvo acquired Anokiwave (announced 2024-01-31, closed Q1 2024); the team moved into Qorvo's High-Performance Analog segment. The Ku-band silicon beamformer ICs cited below (AWMF-0240/0241, 2025-03, −25% Tx / −14% Rx power) are **ex-Anokiwave, now-Qorvo** products. US beamformer-IC leadership had therefore consolidated from "ADI + Anokiwave" to **ADI + Qorvo** — and then consolidated again (next bullet).
- **Layers 1+2 (the 2026 event): Skyworks absorbed all of Qorvo.** The **Skyworks–Qorvo combination completed 2026-10-05** (announced 2025-10; ~**US$22B**; each Qorvo share → US$32.50 cash + 0.960 Skyworks shares; legacy Skyworks holders ~63% / former Qorvo ~37%; keeps the **Skyworks name + SWKS ticker**, CEO Phil Brace; ≥US$500M targeted annual cost savings). Because Qorvo already spanned *both* the beamformer-IC (L1, ex-Anokiwave) and GaN-PA (L2, captive GaN) layers, the US merchant-RF-front-end stack above ADI and MACOM is now **inside a single ~US$22B house (Skyworks)**. Both companies were historically Apple-RFFE-heavy (≈65% / ≈40% of revenue respectively at announcement), so part of the strategic logic is diversifying *toward* the defense/aerospace/SATCOM content this map tracks — Qorvo's High-Performance Analog had already guided **defense-&-aerospace to ≈US$500M for FY2027, surpassing Android** (HPA +50.1% YoY to US$206.3M in Q1 FY2027). *(Note for the reader: as a pending/closed-combination consequence, Qorvo discontinued standalone earnings calls and forward guidance before the close, so post-2026-07 Qorvo-specific segment figures now surface only via Skyworks.)*

Net effect: US front-end leadership is **unchanged at the national level but materially more concentrated** — the ADI + Qorvo beamformer duopoly and the Qorvo-captive-GaN layer are now **ADI + Skyworks(-Qorvo)**, with MACOM the merchant GaN-on-SiC/GaN-on-Si IDM beside them. This is the "GaN-IDM in-sourcing" risk (flagged on [[entities/win-semiconductors]]) playing out on the US side in its sharpest form yet: a domestic GaN-on-SiC RF fab (MACOM/RTP) pulled under one IDM's roof, and the merchant beamformer+GaN design stack (Qorvo) pulled under another ~$22B roof (Skyworks).

**Correction (2026-11-13) — OMMIC is not an independent "Europe" node; it is MACOM's.** Earlier versions of this map (and the Europe row below) listed **OMMIC** (French 100-nm GaN/GaAs foundry, Limeil-Brévannes near Paris) alongside UMS as a standalone European GaN house. In fact **MACOM completed the asset acquisition of OMMIC SAS on 2023-05-31** (the "MACOM European Semiconductor Center" / MESC acquisition, ~US$36.9M). So MACOM runs **two fabs on two substrate platforms in two regions**: RTP (NC) GaN-on-**SiC** and MESC/OMMIC (France) higher-frequency GaN-on-**Si** + GaAs. This matters for the falsifier below: the leading *space-grade Ka-band GaN-on-Si* work runs on OMMIC's D01GH process — i.e. inside a **US IDM's European fab**, not an independent European competitor. The only genuinely independent European merchant GaN foundry left in the top tier is therefore **UMS** (Franco-German), plus the IDMs Infineon/STMicro.

## Layer 2 gets a second axis — the GaN substrate split (GaN-on-SiC vs GaN-on-Si) (加深探究, verified 2026-09-19)

Layer 2 in the stack table is written "GaN-on-SiC (>GaAs >LDMOS)," but by 2026 the GaN PA layer is really **two substrate platforms with a sharp cost/thermal division of labour** — the layer-down mechanism the map had collapsed into one row:

| Substrate | Cost / wafer | Thermal | Where it wins | Where it loses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GaN-on-SiC** | High (SiC substrate) | **Best** — SiC ~3× the thermal conductivity of Si; 2–4 W/mm at Ka-band (26–40 GHz) | High-power, high-EIRP, thermally-stressed: **radar T/R, high-EIRP SATCOM, space-grade** | Cost per PA in high-volume terminals |
| **GaN-on-Si** | **~2× lower** — standard 8-inch (200 mm) Si wafers on CMOS-class lines | Worse (Si ~1/3 the SiC thermal conductivity) → power-density-limited | **Cost-dominated mmWave volume**: 5G massive-MIMO, cost-sensitive flat-panel terminals; 0.1 µm GaN-on-Si at 24–30 GHz in active development (notably by Chinese institutions targeting large-volume 5G) | Peak power density / thermal headroom |

The strategic reading: the **same GaN-IDM in-sourcing move** now splits by end-market. **[[entities/macom|MACOM]] explicitly runs *both* platforms** — GaN-on-SiC (PURE CARBIDE®) for the A&D/high-EIRP tier and GaN-on-Si for cost-driven volume — as does the broader US/Europe merchant tier; the substrate is chosen per application, not per vendor. This *widens* the Layer-2 competitive surface: China's sovereign-by-sanction build-out can attack the **GaN-on-Si volume** end (cheap Si wafers, CMOS-class fabs, 5G-scale demand) without matching SiC-substrate quality, exactly where merchant-foundry margin is thinnest. It also sharpens the [[entities/win-semiconductors]] risk note — the 100-year "GaN-IDM in-sourcing" threat is not one threat but two, and the cheaper (GaN-on-Si) one is the more contestable. Current MACOM product anchors (SATShow Week 2026-03 + IMS 2026-06): **CMPA2H3B025D** 25 W GaN-on-SiC Ka-band SATCOM PA (27–31 GHz, bare die + Cu flange) and L/S-band T/R front-ends (1 W, >40 dB gain, >40% efficiency, PURE CARBIDE® GaN). On the Layer-1 beamformer side, Qorvo added **AWMF-0247** for *shared-aperture* flat-panel arrays (LEO/airborne, power-constrained) above the AWMF-0240 (Rx)/0241 (Tx) Ku-band pair, which reached **volume production Q1 2026** (−25% Tx / −14% Rx DC power). At IMS 2026 (verified 2026-10-10) Qorvo demonstrated the AWMF-0247 as a **Ku-band TDD beamformer** integrating **4 Tx + 4 Rx channels on one die** with factory digital calibration (very low channel-to-channel variation measured live) — the shared-aperture TDD part is the single-IC answer to flat-panel terminal cost. Qorvo also showed a distinct Layer-2-adjacent category, the **QPK1520/QPK1521/QPK1522 sub-100 ns GaN bias controllers** for radar phased arrays (the fast gate/drain-bias sequencer that pulses a GaN PA on/off per pulse) and the **QPA1722** GaN PA (3× instantaneous bandwidth in a 38%-smaller footprint) — a reminder that the GaN-PA layer ships with its own control/bias silicon, not just the amplifier die.

### Why the GaN-on-Si threat is real at sub-6 GHz but still research-grade at mmWave (加深探究 — the falsifier's mechanism, verified 2026-10-10; Ka-band space-grade re-check 2026-11-13)

The cheaper GaN-on-Si substrate is the more contestable end of Layer 2 (falsifier below), but the contest has a **frequency boundary** the one-row split understated. GaN-on-Si's weakness is thermal: Si conducts heat at ~1/3 the rate of SiC, and heat-flux density **rises with frequency** (a Ka-band PA dissipates the same power through a far smaller die than a sub-6 PA). So GaN-on-Si's thermal ceiling bites *hardest exactly where mmWave SATCOM needs it*. The evidence mix as of 2026-10:

- **Sub-6 GHz = commercially proven.** The reference event is the **MACOM–Goertek GaN-on-Si RF joint venture, announced 2019-04-24** (Hong Kong–based, up to US$134.6M consideration) explicitly to supply GaN-on-Si RF power into **China's 5G *base-station* market** — i.e. sub-6 GHz macro/micro cells, where GaN-on-Si on 8-inch Si lines already undercuts GaN-on-SiC on cost. This is the real, dated precedent that the "cheap-substrate volume play" rests on.
- **Ka-band / mmWave = space-grade *demonstrated*, not yet volume-qualified (upgraded 2026-11-13).** The prior "academic/prototype" read now understates the maturity: a **2025 IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory & Techniques paper** (Ramella et al., Politecnico di Torino + Tor Vergata + Bologna, ESA-funded) reports a **space-grade Ka-band GaN-on-Si MMIC PA for SAR altimetry** — 35.5–36 GHz, **10 W** output at 11.25 V drain (8.4 W at 10 V, 6.6 W at 9 V), on **OMMIC's D01GH 100-nm GaN-on-Si process** (now MACOM's, see correction above). This is a genuine step up from the 2021–2023 ~4 W GaN-on-Si Ka-band prototypes and the 2024 GaN-on-Si Ka-band Doherty downlink PA (17.3–20.3 GHz, ~36 dBm, whose process was explicitly *not space-qualified at design time*). **But the paper also *confirms the thermal mechanism this map predicts*, rather than falsifying it:** the device must be de-biased from 11.25 V (10 W) down to **9 V (6.6 W)** specifically to keep the junction under the derated **160 °C** space limit — i.e. Si's ~1/3 thermal conductivity costs ~34% of peak power to stay inside the thermal envelope, exactly the "heat-flux density bites hardest at mmWave" effect. And it remains a **single ESA-funded space-grade *design*, not a high-volume qualified commercial SATCOM product line.** Chinese 0.1 µm 24–30 GHz GaN-on-Si is in development but no qualified high-volume mmWave part was found.

Net: the GaN-on-Si substrate has *already* taken the sub-6 base-station volume (where thermal headroom is slack) and has now reached a **space-grade Ka-band MMIC demonstration** (2025, ESA/OMMIC) — but the **Ka-band SATCOM tier Taiwan and the US front-end vendors actually fight over is still GaN-on-SiC's at volume**, and the space-grade GaN-on-Si demonstration *pays the predicted thermal penalty* (10 W → 6.6 W to meet the 160 °C derating). The falsifier is therefore **still not triggered as of 2026-11** — it fires only if GaN-on-Si reaches Ka-band power-density/reliability parity *at high volume*, which the public record does not yet show; the 2025 result moves the needle on *demonstration maturity*, not on *volume parity*.

## Where Taiwan sits (the honest read)

Taiwan's strength is concentrated in **Layer 2 (and adjacent passives)**: [[entities/win-semiconductors]] is a world-class GaAs/GaN MMIC foundry, [[entities/ascend-tech]] supplies filters/waveguides into Starlink/Kuiper, and [[entities/huatong-pcb]] holds large LEO-PCB share. But Taiwan has **no indigenous Layer-1 beamformer-IC product line, no fighter-grade array integration, and an incomplete Layer-5 radiation-qualification chain** (heavy-ion SEE still requires LBNL/TRIUMF travel per [[concepts/taiwan-radiation-test-ecosystem]]). This is the *exact same shape* as the system-level gap in [[synthesis/leo-taiwan-odc-gap]]: Taiwan is a high-margin **supplier of the hardest-to-make atoms**, not an owner of the integrated product. The investment/strategy implication is identical — the value capture sits one layer up (integration + certification) from where Taiwan currently plays.

## Falsifiability / what would change this read

- If a Taiwanese fabless house ships a competitive SiGe/CMOS SATCOM beamformer IC (Layer 1) at volume → the "L1 absent" claim is falsified; watch Win Semi's foundry customers and local fabless RF startups.
- If Taiwan's superconducting-cyclotron + heavy-ion qualification ([[concepts/taiwan-radiation-test-ecosystem]]) reaches routine flight-part throughput → the Layer-5 gate closes domestically.
- If China's CETC/[[entities/ada-space]] commercial flat-panel terminals reach cost parity with US silicon → the "China = scale, US = silicon-IP" split compresses.
- If GaN-on-Si (cheap 8-inch Si wafers) reaches Ka-band power density and reliability parity with GaN-on-SiC at high volume → the SiC-substrate thermal-headroom moat erodes at the mmWave-volume end, and the merchant-foundry margin most exposed to China's sovereign build-out is the first to compress (watch MACOM/OMMIC + Chinese 0.1 µm 24–30 GHz GaN-on-Si volume qualification). **Status 2026-11: NOT triggered, but demonstration maturity advanced** — GaN-on-Si is commercially proven at volume only at sub-6 GHz base-stations (MACOM–Goertek JV 2019); at Ka-band it has now reached a **space-grade MMIC demonstration** (2025 IEEE TMTT, ESA/OMMIC-D01GH, 10 W @ 36 GHz for SAR), a step beyond the earlier ~4 W prototypes — *but* that demonstration must de-bias 10 W → 6.6 W to meet the 160 °C junction derating, confirming the Si-thermal penalty, and it is a single ESA design, not a high-volume qualified line. Trigger still requires *volume* Ka-band parity, not a demonstrator.
