## Figure of Merit (FOM)

Formula (documented for future runs):

```
FOM = 0.4 × confidence
    + 0.3 × normalized_sizing_sigma
    + 0.2 × recent_hit_rate
    + 0.1 × news_momentum
```

Where each component is normalized to [0, 1]:
- `confidence`: LLM thesis confidence from the scan agent (0–1 direct)
- `normalized_sizing_sigma`: (sizing_sigma − min_sigma) / (max_sigma − min_sigma) across the watchlist
- `recent_hit_rate`: rolling 5-day directional accuracy for this ticker (starts at 0.5 = no-prior-data)
- `news_momentum`: normalized count of positive/negative news signals from the news_scout agent (0–1 scale)
