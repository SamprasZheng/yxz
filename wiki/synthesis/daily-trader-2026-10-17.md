## FOM Formula (for future implementation)

Once the pipeline exists, the intended FOM per ticker is:

```
FOM = 0.4 × confidence
    + 0.3 × normalized_sizing_sigma
    + 0.2 × recent_hit_rate
    + 0.1 × news_momentum
```

Each component normalized to [0, 1] before weighting.

| Component | Weight | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `confidence` | 40% | LLM thesis confidence from `trader scan` output |
| `normalized_sizing_sigma` | 30% | Position-sizing signal from scan, normalized across watchlist |
| `recent_hit_rate` | 20% | Rolling N-day backtest hit rate (direction correct) |
| `news_momentum` | 10% | Sentiment/volume proxy from news_scout agent |
